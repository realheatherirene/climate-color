Climate Color

a Kinfeather project

Free learning experiences that help you find your place in the climate movement.

Climate Color starts with something you already have: the ways you show up for people. Every experience is free, needs no account, and works on a phone or a computer.

  • The Trail

    Climate Color Trail

    Pick one of six trails, each a way of helping. Explore climate topics close to home, and leave with a Field Guide made for you. About 20 minutes.

    Start the Trail →

    Climate Color Wheel

    Learn how the six ways of helping fit together and support each other. Made for groups.
