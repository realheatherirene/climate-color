Climate Color: Core Reference

This page shows the one true copy of the color system. Every value here is read live from the files in core/, so if this page looks right, the source is right. Nothing on this page is typed in by hand.

Colors bring people in; archetypes make people understand, and stay.

The wheel

The Stewardship Wheel, clockwise from the top. Each wedge shows its color with its archetype beneath. The brand wheel adds each color's three words in the outer ring.

Show

The download is whichever wheel is showing. SVG stays sharp at any size; the PNG is 2400 pixels square with a clear background.

The six colors

In list order, starting at Red. Words in a brand color on white use its darker text shade, which must reach at least 4.5 to 1. Pill words use the text shade mixed 88% with black, on an 8% tint of the color. The wheel label is the black or white text on each wedge. The wheel shades are the outer ring behind each color's words, lightest to darkest going clockwise; the words must reach 4.5 to 1 on all three.

Accessibility checks

Every page reads its colors and words from core/. These checks confirm the text on the wheel and in each color's text shade stays readable (at least 4.5 to 1), so a palette change can't quietly break it.
